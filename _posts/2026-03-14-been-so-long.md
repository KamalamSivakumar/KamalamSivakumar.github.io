## net positive change

It has been a long time since I last posted a blog. Life has changed so much this past year in a net positive way. I became an aunt, I moved to New York to pursue my Master's, and lost some friends and made new ones. Net positive way is the key aspect here, net exciting would be a more appropriate description. Not everything changed in perfect ways, but when I look at the overall direction of life, it feels net positive and that’s enough. I'm doing things that I never imagined I would. I'm pursuing grad school in Engineering, working on improving myself in a field that is relatively new to me. I'm incredibly grateful for this opportunity. Whatever I'm working on right now, whatever effort I'm putting in right now, whatever difficult situations I go through right now will compound and manifest in wonderful ways, that's the hope.  

Somewhere between moving countries and starting grad school again, I began to feel like the main character in my own story. One thing I love about New York is the never ending noise and hustle bustle of things. The chaos of New York streets strangely reminds me of walking in DB Road, with all the vehicles buzzing around me, people whizzing past me while walking, my thoughts clearing up in the noise and me feeling like the main character. I am the main character. I understand why people romanticize living in New York. For a girl from Coimbatore, who never once thought about living in New York, or even Bengaluru for that matter, doing engineering in New York feels utterly empowering and humbling at the same time. It took a village for me to get here — my family and loved ones. I miss them very much. Everybody else was a lesson learned in some ways. I'm deeply grateful to all of the experiences that led me here.

Somehow life expanded in ways I didn’t know to plan for.  
I’m simply grateful and kushi to be here, living a life that young Kamalam would be incredibly kushi about.  
I just wanted to write this down and leave it somewhere on the internet forever. :)
